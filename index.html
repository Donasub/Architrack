FAQ

Common questions.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes — 2 active projects, no credit card, no time limit. We only ask you to upgrade when you take on your third project.

Which agencies do you support?

Free covers LASBCA. Professional adds FCDA (Abuja), RSMURD (Rivers) and KANUPDA (Kano), with more states being added quarterly.

Can my client see all my files?

No — the client portal is a clean, simplified view. They see project status and major milestones, never the raw drawings or technical reports.

How do I cancel?

From Settings → Billing, anytime. We'll keep your data accessible for 90 days in case you change your mind.
